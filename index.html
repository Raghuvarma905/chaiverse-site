From leaf to cup

Five steps. No shortcuts.

01

Pluck

Two leaves and a bud, hand-picked at dawn from Assam and Nilgiri estates we buy from directly.

02

Roll & Roast

Leaves are withered and CTC-rolled. Coffee cherries are sun-dried and roasted in 12 kg batches.

03

Pound

Ginger, cardamom, clove, cinnamon and pepper, hand-pounded in a brass imam-dasta every morning.

04

Brew

Four-minute simmer for chai. 9-bar, 27-second shots for espresso. Timed, tasted, every batch.

05

Serve

Poured into a fresh clay kulhad or warmed ceramic, and handed over with a smile, in under 4 minutes.
